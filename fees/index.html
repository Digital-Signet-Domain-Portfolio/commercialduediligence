Commercial Due Diligence Cost UK

What CDD costs where the price is on the public record, and what drives a private quote.

§ DIRECT ANSWER

No commercial due diligence adviser publishes a fee scale: every private mandate is priced in the engagement letter. The prices that are public are UK government contract awards. The National Wealth Fund published five commercial due diligence awards between October 2024 and December 2025, at £41,500 to £300,000 per contract; three of the five were £215,000 or more. Those are investment-deal mandates for a public lender, so read them as reference points, not a rate card for a private-equity bolt-on.

Mixed technical, tax and staffing contracts are left out.

§ 1.2Reading the awards

  • The same buyer paid £41,500 for one mandate and £300,000 for another within five months. Scope moves the price far more than the label “commercial due diligence” does.
  • A Big-4 firm (Ernst & Young) and a specialist (Analysys Mason) each took a £215,000 award from the same buyer. The awards do not show one tier as consistently cheaper.
  • National Wealth Fund deals are infrastructure and energy investments. A private-equity buyer on a smaller bolt-on is a different client buying a different scope, so its quote can sit well outside this range in either direction.

§ 1.3What drives a private CDD quote

DriverPushes the fee up when
DD typeFull seven-chapter scope rather than red-flag; buy-side work from scratch rather than a top-up off a vendor report
Primary researchMore customer and competitor interviews, a commissioned survey, more than one geography
RelianceLenders or a syndicate need a reliance letter; liability caps negotiated upward
TimelineCompressed delivery on a competitive process; a second-round refresh
SectorRegulated or specialist markets (healthcare, financial services) that need sector partners

Ask every adviser to price the same written scope, and compare against the scope template. For who to approach, see the providers index and Big-4 vs boutique.
