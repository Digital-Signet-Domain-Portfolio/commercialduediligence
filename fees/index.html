Commercial Due Diligence Cost UK

UK CDD fee bands by deal size, DD type and provider tier. Quote-only mandates marked as such. No inferred per-day rates.

§ DIRECT ANSWER

UK commercial due diligence cost clusters in two visible bands. Boutique mid-market mandates run £15-25k for red-flag and up to £50k for full scope, delivered in 3-5 weeks. Big-4 deal-advisory mandates run £75-150k and up to £250k for upper-mid-market full scope, delivered in 5-8 weeks. These are indicative market-practice ranges, not a published rate card: no CDD firm publishes fees, and every mandate is priced in the engagement letter.

§ 1.3What is included

A £20k boutique mandate usually covers: 10-15 customer interviews, a market-sizing chapter, a competitive map, a one-page risk register and a draft IC summary. Excludes operations diligence in depth, second-round customer follow-ups and partner-level cross-border coverage. A £120k Big-4 mandate covers everything in the boutique scope plus deeper operations diligence, syndicated-lender-acceptable reliance, and a partner cover letter readers expect on a buy-side syndication.
