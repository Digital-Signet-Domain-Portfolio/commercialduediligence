VC2.

OC&C vs L.E.K. Commercial Due Diligence

Side-by-side compare: OC&C Strategy Consultants and L.E.K. Consulting on UK mid-market CDD.

§ DIRECT ANSWER

OC&C wins consumer and retail; L.E.K. wins healthcare and life sciences. Both upper-mid-market boutiques; both quote-only, neither publishes a rate card. Decision is sector fit, not fee spread. Source: firm public material.
